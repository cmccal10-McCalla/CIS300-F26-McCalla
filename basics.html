Basics of Animation

What exactly is Animation?

Animation is process of stringing together successive drawings to create an illusion of movement. Pictures are usually drawn on transparent celluoid sheets to be photographed. However, more modern methods include creating digital illustrations on drawing tablets.There are many ways in which animation is created including 2D-rigging, stop-motion, and CGI.
